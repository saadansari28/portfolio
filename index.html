01

Credit Card Portfolio
Analysis

3-Page Power BI Dashboard

Built a 3-page Power BI report analyzing 10,000+ customer credit card records, covering transaction trends, customer segmentation, and credit risk. Designed a relational data model in PostgreSQL, linking customer and transaction tables via foreign keys, and built custom DAX measures for per-customer acquisition cost, delinquency rate, and utilization-based risk segmentation. The analysis uncovered a mismatch between card-tier acquisition cost and revenue, and a counterintuitive relationship between credit utilization and delinquency rate. Also simulated a real-world data refresh pipeline by appending new records in SQL and validating end-to-end updates in Power BI.
